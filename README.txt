WISHES GIFT DELUXE
===================
Files:
- index.html
- style.css
- script.js
- assets/ (your six uploaded pictures + gentle-melody.wav)

Preview:
Open index.html in a browser. For full browser behavior, use Live Server in VS Code.

Deploy to Netlify:
1. Extract this ZIP.
2. Open https://app.netlify.com/drop and sign in.
3. Upload the extracted wishes_gift_deluxe folder (or drag the folder contents as Netlify requests).
4. Open the Netlify URL it gives you.

Notes:
- The melody is an original, locally generated gentle synthesized tune and is included as assets/gentle-melody.wav.
- Browsers generally require a tap before playing audio.
- The Agree choice advances to the plan pages. Disagree on the invitation does not advance.
- Selecting “Dislike” for ice cream will not let the visitor continue until they select a flavour.
- The date and weekday are checked for consistency.
